The Dual-Optics Engineering Philosophy

In standard World of Warcraft, spanning across two monitors forces your character directly behind the plastic monitor bezel and stretches your 3D camera into a nauseating horizontal fisheye.

Akimbo uses custom trigonometric viewport coordinate transformations to isolate the 3D rendering pipeline to your gaming monitor. Your camera stays centered, your FOV stays crisp, and your second screen becomes a dedicated tactical workstation.

3 Simple Steps to Dual-Screen Glory

No complex configuration required. Get up and running in under two minutes.

1

Install In-Game Addon

Search for Akimbo in the CurseForge App, WowUp, or download it manually into your World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\ directory.

2

Span the Window

With WoW set to Windowed mode, run the included Akimbo-Span.bat (or use Borderless Gaming / DisplayFusion) to stretch the window across both displays.

3

Calibrate with /akimbo wizard

In chat, type /akimbo wizard and click 1-Click Auto-Configure. Akimbo snaps the 3D world to your gaming screen and opens your command deck.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, compatibility, and setup.

Is Akimbo 100% Blizzard ToS compliant?

Yes, absolutely. The in-game addon is written entirely in standard World of Warcraft Lua using Blizzard's official frame management and viewport APIs. The desktop companion simply calls standard Windows desktop positioning to size the borderless window. It contains zero memory reading, zero injection, and zero automation.

Does it work with different monitor sizes and refresh rates?

Yes. Akimbo was specifically built to handle asymmetrical setups, such as a 1440x2560 vertical portrait monitor paired with a 2560x1440 curved gaming monitor, as well as matching dual landscape monitors. Different refresh rates (e.g. 144Hz + 60Hz) run smoothly in Windows borderless mode.

Can I use Borderless Gaming or DisplayFusion instead?

Yes! The Akimbo addon only requires that the WoW window spans both physical screens. If you already use Borderless Gaming on Steam, DisplayFusion, or custom window managers, you don't even need our script — just span WoW and type /akimbo wizard.

Which versions of World of Warcraft are supported?

Akimbo supports Classic Era (1.15.x), Season of Discovery, Hardcore, Cataclysm Classic, and modern Retail (The War Within). The core layout engine is compatible across all versions.
